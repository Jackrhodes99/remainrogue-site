PRODUCT PHOTOS — DO THIS BEFORE YOU CANCEL WIX
=============================================

Right now the website shows your photos straight from Wix. If you cancel
your Wix account, Wix may delete them and the photos on your new site will
break. Follow these steps first. It takes about 15 minutes.

STEP 1  Save each photo
  - Open each link below in your browser.
  - Right-click the photo (on a phone or Mac trackpad: press and hold, or
    Control-click) and choose "Save Image As...".
  - Save it with the exact "Save as" name shown. Names must match exactly,
    including lower-case letters and the .jpg / .png ending.
  - Tip: if you still have the original photos on your phone or computer,
    use those instead. They're usually better quality. Make each one
    smaller than 1 MB (around 1500 px wide is plenty).

STEP 2  Upload them into this "images" folder on GitHub
  - In your GitHub repository, click the "images" folder, then
    "Add file" > "Upload files". Drag all the photos in, then click
    "Commit changes".

STEP 3  Point products.json at the new files
  - Open products.json in GitHub and click the pencil icon to edit.
  - Replace each long https://static.wixstatic.com/... address with the
    matching "New address" from the list below, for example:
        "/images/mojito-1.jpg"
  - Click "Commit changes". The site updates in about a minute.

STEP 4  Check the website. Every product photo should still show.
        Only then cancel Wix.
