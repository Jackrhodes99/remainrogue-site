PRODUCT PHOTOS
==============

Done. Your website now uses its own copies of every product photo
(in the folders inside this "images" folder), so it no longer depends on Wix.

Full-size originals are saved on your computer in:
  Documents\Remain Rogue photos\  (one folder per product)

Adding photos for a new product:
  1. Make each photo smaller than 1 MB (about 1600 px wide is plenty).
  2. In GitHub, open the "images" folder, then Add file > Upload files.
  3. Use the photo's address in products.json, e.g. "/images/new-polo-1.jpg".
