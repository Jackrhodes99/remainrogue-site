About us

Remain Rogue is a bold, modern golf clothing brand designed for players who challenge the norms both on and off the course.

We combine performance-driven materials with sharp, confident style for golfers who value individuality as much as precision in their game. Our pieces are made to move from the fairway to everyday life without missing a beat.

The brand is for the players who break boundaries, defy expectations, and stay unapologetically themselves. Polos that get noticed, tees with a sense of humour, and caps built to handle the weather.

Based in Queensland

Remain Rogue is an Australian business based in Queensland. We pack and ship every order ourselves, Australia-wide and internationally.
